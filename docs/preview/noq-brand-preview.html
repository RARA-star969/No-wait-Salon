03 — The system

Fixed Brand Zone vs. Dynamic Category Zone

Every screen is built from two zones. The split is what keeps NOQ recognizable no matter which category a customer is browsing.

🔒 Brand Zone — always NOQ

  • Header — location pill, wallet chip, avatar
  • Search shell — glass surface, icon, placeholder rhythm
  • Bottom navigation — icons, labels, active-state color
  • QR center button — always brand violet, always raised
  • Typography system — Sora display, Inter body
  • Base dark glass surfaces — #0F1116 / #171A22

🎨 Category Zone — changes per world

  • Floating deck — front card art, side-card mood
  • Ambient glow — ring color behind the front card
  • Featured banner accent — glow + CTA underline only
  • Listing highlights — a 2px top border, nothing more
  • Chips / badges / icons — category tint
04 — Color logic

One brand color. Five category accents.

Category color is never allowed to become the app's identity — it decorates the zone it belongs to and stops there.

05 — Why this works

Solving the "every category owns a color" problem

01

Brand recall stays intact

Right now the header, chips and CTAs shift color with every category tap — a customer never sees a stable "NOQ blue." Locking those to one violet gives the brand something to be remembered by.

02

Category still gets a voice

Gym should feel energetic, Spa should feel calm — that's real product value. This system keeps that personality alive in the deck and banner, just without letting it repaint the whole app.

03

Scales past five categories

Adding a sixth or seventh category later is just one new accent color and one new deck illustration — the shell, nav and typography don't need to be touched.
